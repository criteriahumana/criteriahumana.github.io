About

Before it is a blog, this is a commitment to myself.

I do more and more with the help of language models, and I noticed I was also handing over the part I most wanted to do myself: thinking, reading, researching and writing. This blog is how I set time aside for that. Making it public is what keeps me at it.

I don’t write as an expert. In my day-to-day I am not especially rational or strategic, and writing is how I work on that. If it helps me, I suspect it will help someone else.

What it is about

Trying to understand what is around me: questioning it, asking things of it, trying to explain it. What matters most to me is that process, more than ending up knowing something. When I force myself to explain a thing is when I take it in and really understand it, instead of just knowing about it.

What I am after is judgement: knowing what deserves attention, what to ask, what to believe and what to do next. That takes me to different places. How we make decisions, small and large, and which ideas from strategy or economics help us make them better. How an opinion forms and what ought to change it. How attention, memory and learning work, and what technology is doing to them. Creativity, in music and beyond it. And what is still worth doing yourself now that almost everything can be delegated.

My thesis is that this kind of judgement is antifragile to AI. I may be wrong; writing this is also a way of finding out.

How it is made

The topics, the research and the writing are mine. I try to make each article useful in practice and readable without being a specialist.

I write in Spanish and translate into English.
